I am a lecturer in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang Normal University. I received my Ph.D. from Central South University.

## Research

I work in theoretical computer science, with a focus on parameterized algorithms and approximation algorithms. My current interests include

- Graph modification problems
- Algorithms for block-structured integer programs
- Applications of theoretical computer science to real-world problems
