Welcome to my page! I am from Qingdao, Shandong, and received my Ph.D. from Central South University. My research is in theoretical computer science, with a focus on parameterized algorithms and approximation algorithms. I am currently a lecturer in the College of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang Normal University. My research interests include graph modification problems, solving block-structured integer programs, and real-world applications of theoretical computer science. If you have an interesting problem, I would love to discuss it with you!
